Our Story

Where Every Meal Becomes a Memory

Founded with a simple dream - to serve food that brings people together - Sanjay Caterers has grown into Pune's most beloved catering company. For over three decades, we have been the trusted choice for families, corporates, and event organizers who demand nothing but the finest.

Our team of master chefs, trained in traditional Maharashtrian and pan-Indian cuisines, blend age-old recipes with contemporary presentation - delivering flavours that linger long after the last bite.
